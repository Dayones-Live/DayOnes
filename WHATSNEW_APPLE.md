# What's New in This Version (Apple App Store)

## Version 2.2.6

### Enhanced Signature Editing Experience
• **Expanded Size Options**: Choose from 5 signature sizes including two new smaller options for more precise placement
• **Larger Maximum Sizes**: Increased medium and large signature sizes for better visibility on your photos
• **Original Color Option**: New default color setting lets you use signatures in their original colors without any overlay
• **Improved Signature Controls**: Better size cycling and editing tools for a smoother customization experience
